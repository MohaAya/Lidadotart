I turn ideas into visual identities and digital experiences people remember. From bold brand identities to intuitive interfaces, I combine strategy, aesthetics, and function to make every detail count.

Trained as a graphic designer and later earning an MBA in Marketing, I bring a strategic eye to every project I touch. Since 2016 I’ve worked as an independent designer, building brand identities and digital experiences for businesses that want their visuals to actually support their goals, not just look nice.

These days I spend most of my time in UI/UX and app design, shaping interfaces, icons, and product experiences that feel intuitive from the first tap. But I never really left branding behind.
